Code reminders: EMT

  • No more than 360° of bends between pull points (boxes, conduit bodies). NEC 358.26
  • Strap within 3 ft of every box, cabinet or fitting, and at least every 10 ft. NEC 358.30
  • Ream every cut end to remove burrs. NEC 358.28
  • Bends must not crush or kink the pipe; keep the bender's radius. NEC 358.24
  • Fill: max 40% for 3 or more wires, 31% for 2, 53% for 1. NEC Ch. 9, Table 1
  • Use raintight fittings in wet locations. NEC 358.42

Quick reminders only. Your local code and inspector have the final word.
